Founder & Lead Clinician

Ahebwa Keisha Patience

Counselling Psychologist · Public Health Specialist

MSc Public Health BSc Counselling Psychology Trauma-Informed Therapy Integrative Wellness 8+ Years Clinical Experience

Ahebwa Keisha Patience is a licensed Counselling Psychologist and Public Health Specialist with over eight years of clinical experience in Kampala. She founded Thrive Therapy & Wellness with a singular vision: to make whole-person wellness accessible to Ugandans — addressing mind, body and hormones together, in one compassionate space.

Her approach is integrative and deeply human. She blends evidence-based psychological therapies with public health insight, helping clients move beyond symptom management toward lasting transformation. She works with individuals, couples, families and organisations.
