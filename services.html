Our Services

At Thrive Therapy & Wellness, we believe wellbeing isn't one-size-fits-all. Our work is built around five integrated pillars — you can start with one, combine a few, or embrace the full whole-person experience.

01
Pillar One

Mental Wellness

Compassionate, evidence-based therapy for individuals, couples and families navigating stress, anxiety, depression, grief and trauma. Every session is confidential and grounded in the ethical standards of the Uganda Counselling Association. This pillar also includes dedicated pre-retirement counselling and post-retirement trauma support — helping long-serving professionals prepare emotionally for the transition out of work, and process its impact once they have left.

Healthy Living & Relationships

Personalised nutrition, movement and lifestyle coaching paired with relationship and family counselling — because your physical health and your closest relationships shape each other. From premarital and marriage counselling to simply building healthier daily habits, this pillar helps you feel stronger, calmer and more connected.

Pillar Four

Children's & Adolescent Development

Guidance for parents and caregivers navigating their children's emotional, behavioural and developmental needs — practical support, parenting workshops, and referral to specialised care when needed. This pillar also focuses on the changes teenagers experience during puberty and how parenting evolves for both the teenager and parent, including support for those difficult conversations.
