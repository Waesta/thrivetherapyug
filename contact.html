FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Is therapy confidential? +

Yes, absolutely. Everything shared in your sessions is strictly confidential between you and your therapist. We only break confidentiality in rare legal exceptions (such as risk of harm to yourself or others).

How do I know which therapist is right for me? +

During your free initial consultation, we assess your needs and match you with the therapist best suited to your situation, preferences, and goals. You can also request to switch therapists at any time.

How many sessions will I need? +

This varies greatly by individual and presenting concern. Some clients see significant progress in 6–8 sessions, while others benefit from ongoing support. Your therapist will discuss a recommended treatment plan with you.

Do you offer online therapy? +

Yes! We offer fully secure online video sessions for clients anywhere in Uganda or internationally. Online sessions are equally effective and offer maximum privacy and convenience.

What are your payment methods? +

We accept cash, MTN Mobile Money, Airtel Money, and bank transfer. Payment is due before each session. We can also arrange payment plans for clients undertaking long-term therapy.
