Licensed couples and family therapists in Kampala helping you communicate better, resolve conflict and build the relationship you deserve.

What Is Couples & Family Therapy?

Couples therapy (also called relationship counselling) is a specialised form of therapy that focuses on the dynamic between two or more people in a relationship. A trained therapist helps partners understand each other better, communicate more effectively and work through challenges — whether you're in crisis or simply want to strengthen a good relationship.

Family therapy looks at the family as a system, exploring how each person's behaviour affects the others, and helping families develop healthier patterns of relating, communicating and supporting each other.

What We Help With

  • Communication breakdown — recurring arguments, misunderstandings, emotional distance
  • Trust & infidelity — rebuilding after betrayal, jealousy, broken trust
  • Intimacy issues — emotional and physical disconnection
  • Premarital counselling — preparation for marriage, conflict prevention
  • Parenting conflicts — disagreements over discipline, co-parenting after separation
  • Blended families — step-parenting, merging households
  • Family grief — navigating loss together as a family
  • Life transitions — new baby, relocation, job change, bereavement

Our Therapeutic Approach

Our couples and family therapists are trained in Gottman Method Couples Therapy, Systemic Family Therapy and Emotionally Focused Therapy (EFT) — among the most evidence-based frameworks available for relationship work.

Sessions are non-judgmental, culturally sensitive to Ugandan family structures and values, and designed to give every person in the room a voice. The goal is not to take sides, but to help you understand each other and find a path forward — together.

Premarital Counselling

Preparing for marriage is one of the best investments you can make. Our premarital programme covers communication styles, conflict management, financial alignment, family-of-origin patterns and shared values — giving you a strong foundation before you begin.

When Both Partners Aren't Ready

Sometimes only one partner is willing to attend therapy at first — that's okay. Individual therapy can help you develop skills and clarity that may eventually bring your partner on board. We work with you wherever you are.
