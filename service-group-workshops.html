Group & Workshops

Heal Together.
Grow Together.

Therapist-led group sessions and community wellness workshops in Kampala. The shared experience of group work is powerful — reducing isolation and building real community.

The Power of Group Therapy

Group therapy is one of the most effective and underutilised forms of psychological support available. Led by a trained therapist, these sessions bring together people navigating similar experiences — anxiety, depression, grief, trauma, burnout — to share, learn and grow together.

Research consistently shows that group therapy is as effective as individual therapy for many conditions, often faster, and offers something individual therapy cannot: the powerful experience of hearing your story reflected in others' lives, of feeling truly understood by people who've been there.

Our Groups & Workshops

Women's Wellness Circle

A safe, recurring group for women to discuss mental health, hormonal wellbeing, relationships, motherhood and personal growth. Facilitated by our therapist and nutrition specialist. Monthly sessions in Ntinda and online.

Anxiety & Stress Management Group

A structured 6-week group therapy programme teaching evidence-based techniques for managing anxiety and chronic stress — breathing, grounding, CBT tools and lifestyle strategies.

Grief & Loss Support Group

A compassionate space for those navigating bereavement, divorce or significant loss. Facilitated by a trauma-informed therapist with lived experience in Ugandan community contexts.

Youth Mental Health Workshops

School-based and community workshops for young people (10–24) covering mental health literacy, emotional regulation, peer relationships and help-seeking behaviour.

Corporate Wellness Workshops

Half-day and full-day workshops for organisations on mental health at work, burnout prevention, communication and building psychologically safe teams. Delivered at your premises across Kampala.

Who Should Attend?

Group therapy and workshops are ideal for anyone who wants the benefits of therapeutic support in a community setting — whether as a supplement to individual therapy or as a standalone experience. They are also significantly more affordable than individual sessions, making them accessible to more Ugandans.
