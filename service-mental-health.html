Therapy in Kampala

Compassionate, evidence-based therapy for individuals, youth, couples and families. Licensed counselling psychologists in Ntinda — in-person and online.

What Is Mental Health Therapy?

Mental health therapy — also called counselling or psychotherapy — is a professional, structured conversation between you and a trained therapist. It helps you understand your thoughts, feelings and behaviours, develop coping strategies and work through challenges that are affecting your daily life, relationships or wellbeing.

At Thrive Therapy & Wellness in Ntinda, Kampala, our therapists provide evidence-based mental health support that is culturally sensitive, confidential and focused on lasting change — not just managing symptoms.

Conditions We Support

  • Anxiety and panic disorders — generalised anxiety, social anxiety, panic attacks
  • Depression — low mood, loss of motivation, persistent sadness, burnout
  • Stress — workplace pressure, academic stress, life transitions
  • Relationship difficulties — communication problems, conflict, separation
  • Grief and loss — bereavement, divorce, job loss
  • Trauma and PTSD — past abuse, accidents, sudden loss
  • Youth and adolescent issues — school pressure, identity, family conflict
  • Self-esteem and identity — confidence, purpose, life direction

Our Approach: Integrative Therapy

We draw from multiple evidence-based frameworks — including Cognitive Behavioural Therapy (CBT), trauma-informed therapy, mindfulness-based approaches and solution-focused therapy — to create a personalised plan that fits your unique situation and goals.

Sessions are available for individuals (adults, teenagers and children with parental consent), couples and groups. Both in-person sessions at our Ntinda clinic and secure online sessions are available.

Individual Therapy

One-on-one sessions with a licensed therapist. A confidential space to explore what's going on, understand its roots and build practical skills to move forward.

Youth & Adolescent Therapy

Specialist support for young people aged 10–24 navigating school pressure, identity, family conflict, peer relationships and mental health challenges unique to Uganda's youth.

Group Therapy

Structured, therapist-led groups for specific issues — anxiety, depression, grief and more. The shared experience of group work is powerful, reducing isolation and building community.

Is Mental Health Therapy Right for Me?

Therapy is for anyone who wants to feel better, understand themselves more deeply or work through something difficult. You don't need to be in crisis to benefit. Many clients come to therapy as a form of regular mental maintenance — much like going to the gym for your body.

Signs it may be time to talk to a therapist include: persistent low mood, anxiety interfering with daily life, relationship problems, feeling stuck or overwhelmed, difficulty sleeping, or a sense that something isn't right even when you can't name it.
