1. Who We Are

Thrive Therapy & Wellness is a professional wellness practice based at Emesa Complex, Opposite Kampala Independent Hospital, Ntinda, Kampala, Uganda. We provide individual, couples, family, and group therapy, alongside physical wellness and hormonal health services.

For the purposes of this Privacy Policy, the data controller is Patience Ahebwa, Clinician, Public Health Specialist, and Counselling Psychologist — Founder and Lead Clinician of Thrive Therapy & Wellness.

If you have any questions about how your data is handled, you can reach us at info@thriveug.com or +256 775 939 636.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information in two ways: information you provide to us directly, and information gathered automatically when you use our website.

Information you provide directly:

  • Your name, email address, and phone number when you complete our contact or booking forms.
  • The nature of your enquiry or the service you are seeking.
  • Appointment scheduling details, including preferred dates and times.
  • Health and wellbeing information shared during sessions or intake assessments — this is necessary to provide you with safe, appropriate, and effective care.
  • Payment information (processed at the point of payment; we do not retain full card details).

Information collected automatically:

  • Website usage data including pages visited, time spent on the site, and general geographic location (country/region level) via Google Analytics.
  • Device type and browser information used for optimising our website experience.
  • Cookie preferences and session data (see Section 6 for full details).

We only collect information that is necessary and relevant to delivering your care or improving our services. We do not collect sensitive data beyond what you choose to share with us.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your information exclusively to provide you with a high-quality, personalised service. Specifically, we use it to:

  • Provide therapy, wellness, and clinical services to you as a client.
  • Confirm, manage, and remind you of upcoming appointments via email, SMS, or phone.
  • Respond to your enquiries promptly and helpfully.
  • Maintain accurate clinical records for the continuity and safety of your care.
  • Send relevant service updates or information, only where you have consented to receive these.
  • Understand how visitors use our website so we can improve it (using anonymised analytics data).
  • Comply with applicable professional, legal, and regulatory obligations.

We will never sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for commercial or marketing purposes.

4. Confidentiality of Therapy Sessions

Confidentiality is the foundation of a safe therapeutic relationship. Everything you share within your therapy sessions — whether in person or online — is strictly confidential. Your personal disclosures, health history, and session content will not be shared with anyone without your explicit written consent.

However, there are a small number of circumstances in which confidentiality may need to be broken. These are limited to situations where the law or professional ethics require it:

  • Risk of serious harm to yourself: If you disclose an immediate and credible intention to end your own life or cause serious harm to yourself, we have a duty of care to act — which may include contacting emergency services or a trusted contact with your knowledge.
  • Risk of serious harm to others: If you disclose a specific, credible threat to harm another person, we are required to take reasonable steps to protect that individual.
  • Court order: If we are compelled by a valid court order or other lawful authority to disclose information, we are legally required to comply.
  • Safeguarding a child or vulnerable adult: If information shared suggests that a child or vulnerable adult is at risk of abuse or neglect, we are obligated to report this to the appropriate authorities in line with Uganda's child protection legislation.

In any of the above situations, we will always aim to discuss this with you first where it is safe to do so, and any disclosures will be limited strictly to what is necessary.

5. Data Storage & Security

We take the security of your personal information seriously. Your data is stored using secure, password-protected systems, and access is restricted to Patience Ahebwa and any directly authorised clinical or administrative staff who require it to support your care.

We implement reasonable technical and organisational safeguards to protect your data against unauthorised access, accidental loss, alteration, or disclosure. These include:

  • Password-protected devices and accounts with strong authentication.
  • Secure, encrypted communication channels for online sessions.
  • Restricted physical access to any paper records held at our clinic.
  • Regular review of our data security practices.

While we take every reasonable precaution, please be aware that no data transmission over the internet is entirely risk-free. If you have concerns about how you communicate with us, please contact us directly to discuss secure options.

We will never sell, trade, or otherwise transfer your personal information to third parties without your consent, except as described in this policy.

6. Cookies & Analytics

Our website uses cookies — small text files stored on your device — to help it function properly and to understand how visitors use the site.

Types of cookies we use:

  • Essential cookies: Required for the website to load and function. These cannot be disabled without affecting site performance.
  • Analytics cookies (Google Analytics): We use Google Analytics to understand aggregate, anonymised patterns in how visitors use our website — such as which pages are most visited and how long people spend on the site. This data does not identify you personally and helps us improve the site experience.

Google Analytics uses anonymised IP addresses in line with its own privacy standards. You can opt out of Google Analytics tracking at any time by installing the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

You can also manage or disable cookies through your browser settings at any time. Note that disabling certain cookies may affect how our website performs for you.

7. Your Rights

You have clear rights over your personal data. Specifically, you have the right to:

  • Access your data: Request a copy of the personal information we hold about you.
  • Correct your data: Ask us to correct any inaccurate or incomplete information.
  • Delete your data: Request that we delete your personal information, subject to any legal or professional obligations we have to retain records (see Section 8).
  • Object to processing: Object to the use of your data for specific purposes, including direct marketing communications.
  • Withdraw consent: Where you have provided consent for us to contact you, you may withdraw this at any time.
  • Data portability: Request that your data be provided to you in a structured, commonly used format.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at info@thriveug.com or call +256 775 939 636. We will respond to all requests within a reasonable time and in accordance with applicable law.

8. Data Retention

We retain client records for a minimum of 7 years after the conclusion of your last session, in line with professional clinical guidelines and ethical obligations for psychological and health service records.

After this period, records will be securely deleted or destroyed. If you were a minor at the time of treatment, records will be retained until you reach the age of 25 or for 7 years after your last session — whichever is later.

Website contact form submissions and general enquiry data not resulting in an ongoing client relationship will be retained for a maximum of 2 years, after which they will be deleted.

Where you request deletion of your data before the standard retention period has elapsed, we will consider your request carefully. In some cases, our professional duties may require us to retain a minimum record; we will explain this clearly if it applies.

9. Third-Party Services

To operate our website and communications effectively, we use a small number of trusted third-party services. Each operates under its own privacy policy and security standards:

  • EmailJS — Used to process messages submitted through our contact form. Your submitted information is transmitted via EmailJS servers. Please review the EmailJS Privacy Policy.
  • Google Analytics — Used to understand how visitors use our website through anonymised, aggregate data. Please review the Google Privacy Policy.
  • Vercel — Our website is hosted on Vercel's platform, which processes web requests in order to serve you this site. Please review the Vercel Privacy Policy.

We do not use advertising networks, social media tracking pixels, or any data brokers on this website. We will not share your data with third parties beyond what is described above without your explicit consent.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices, services, or applicable law. When we do, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page.

We encourage you to review this page periodically so that you remain informed about how we are protecting your information. Continued use of our website or services after any changes constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.

If we make significant changes that affect how we use your personal data, we will make reasonable efforts to notify you directly — for example, by email if you are an active client.
