About Thrive Therapy & Wellness

Where Mental And Physical Wellness Meet — guided by compassion, science, and a medication-free approach to lasting health.

Our Story

Founded on the Belief That You Can Reboot Your Life

Thrive Therapy & Wellness was created with a single, powerful vision: that every person deserves access to comprehensive, whole-body care — without stigma, without barriers, and without over-reliance on medication.

Founded by Ahebwa Keisha Patience — Counselling Psychologist and Public Health Specialist — Thrive Therapy integrates mental health, physical strength, hormonal wellness and nutrition into one holistic, multi-clinician practice.

We serve clients in person at our clinic in Ntinda, Kampala and through flexible online sessions — bringing world-class care to every corner of Uganda.

Founder & Lead Clinician

Ahebwa Keisha Patience

Counselling Psychologist · Public Health Specialist · Founder of Thrive Therapy & Wellness

Ahebwa Keisha Patience is the founder and lead clinician of Thrive Therapy & Wellness. With formal training spanning clinical medicine, public health and counselling psychology, she brings a uniquely integrated perspective to wellness — one that addresses mind, body and hormones as one interconnected system.

Patience believes in a Medication-Free Rich Life Approach — helping clients address the root causes of their challenges rather than simply managing symptoms. Her sessions blend evidence-based therapy with lifestyle, hormonal and nutritional support.

She is especially passionate about women's health, hormonal wellbeing and breaking mental health stigma across Uganda. She also leads the Thrive team of specialist clinicians, ensuring every client receives the right care from the right practitioner.

Our Approach

Thrive Therapy addresses every dimension of your health for truly lasting change.

Mental & Emotional Wellness
