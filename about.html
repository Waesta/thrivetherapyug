About Thrive Therapy UG

A team of compassionate, licensed professionals dedicated to your mental wellness journey.

Our Story

Healing at the Heart of Everything We Do

Thrive Therapy UG was founded with a single, powerful belief: that every Ugandan deserves access to quality mental health care — without stigma, without barriers, and with deep cultural understanding.

Since 2016, we have walked alongside hundreds of individuals, couples, and families through their most difficult seasons. Our approach blends evidence-based therapeutic methods with a warm, human touch that honours the whole person.

We are proud to serve clients across Uganda — in our Kampala clinic and through flexible online sessions.
