About Thrive Therapy & Wellness

Where Mental And Physical Wellness Meet — guided by compassion, science, and a medication-free approach to lasting health.

Our Story

Founded on the Belief That You Can Reboot Your Life

Thrive Therapy & Wellness was created with a single, powerful vision: that every person deserves access to comprehensive, whole-body care — without stigma, without barriers, and without over-reliance on medication.

Founded and led by Patience Ahebwa — a Clinician, Public Health Specialist, and Counselling Psychologist — Thrive Therapy integrates mental health, physical strength, hormonal wellness, and nutrition into one holistic practice.

We serve clients in person at our clinic in Ntinda, Kampala and through flexible online sessions — bringing world-class care to every corner of Uganda.

Meet the Founder

Patience Ahebwa

Clinician · Public Health Specialist · Counselling Psychologist

Patience Ahebwa is the heart and driving force behind Thrive Therapy & Wellness. With formal training spanning clinical medicine, public health, and counselling psychology, she brings a uniquely integrated perspective to wellness.

Patience believes in a Medication-Free Rich Life Approach — helping clients address the root causes of their challenges rather than simply managing symptoms. Her sessions blend evidence-based therapy with lifestyle, hormonal, and nutritional support.

She is especially passionate about women's health, hormonal wellbeing, and breaking mental health stigma across Uganda.

Our Values

What Guides Our Work

Holistic Care

We treat the whole person — mind, body, and hormones — not just the presenting symptom.

Medication-Free Approach

Our goal is a rich, full life — achieved through therapy, lifestyle, and natural healing wherever possible.

Cultural Sensitivity

We honour the unique cultural backgrounds and lived experiences of every Ugandan client.

Confidentiality

Your privacy is sacred. Everything shared in sessions is strictly confidential.

Our Approach

Three Pillars of Wellness

Thrive Therapy addresses every dimension of your health for truly lasting change.

Mental & Emotional Wellness

Individual, couples, family, and group therapy. Counselling for anxiety, depression, trauma, grief, addiction, and life transitions.

Physical Strength & Vitality

Personalised fitness, nutrition guidance, and lifestyle coaching to build a stronger, more energised body.

Hormonal & Holistic Health

Specialist support for perimenopause, PCOS, menstrual health, and sexual & reproductive wellbeing.
