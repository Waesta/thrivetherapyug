Hormonal Health

Your Hormones Are
Trying to Tell You Something

PCOS, perimenopause, irregular cycles and hormonal imbalances — our specialists help you understand your body and restore balance, naturally and sustainably.

Understanding Hormonal Imbalance

Your hormones are chemical messengers that regulate virtually every system in your body — mood, energy, metabolism, reproduction, sleep and more. When they fall out of balance, the effects can be wide-ranging and deeply disruptive: weight gain that won't respond to diet, persistent fatigue, mood swings, irregular periods, skin changes and loss of libido.

At Thrive Therapy & Wellness, our hormonal health coaching is led by clinicians who understand both the science of hormones and the lived reality of Ugandan women navigating these challenges — often without adequate support.

Conditions We Support

  • PCOS (Polycystic Ovary Syndrome) — insulin resistance, irregular cycles, hair loss, acne
  • Perimenopause — hot flashes, night sweats, mood changes, sleep disruption, weight gain
  • Post-menopause — bone health, cardiovascular risk, mood stabilisation
  • Thyroid disorders — supporting lifestyle management alongside medical care
  • Adrenal fatigue & cortisol imbalance — chronic stress and its hormonal effects
  • Menstrual irregularities — heavy periods, painful cycles, PMS, amenorrhoea
  • Endometriosis — lifestyle and dietary support alongside medical management
  • Post-partum hormonal shifts — including post-partum depression support

Our Approach: Root-Cause, Not Symptom-Management

Rather than masking symptoms with medication, we work to identify and address the underlying drivers of hormonal imbalance — through targeted nutrition, lifestyle modification, stress management, sleep optimisation and, where appropriate, evidence-based supplementation.

This approach works alongside — not instead of — any medical treatment you may be receiving. We coordinate with your doctor to ensure integrated care.

PCOS Programme

A structured, multi-session programme specifically for women with PCOS covering: insulin management through food, cycle tracking, exercise selection, supplement protocols and stress reduction — delivered by our nutrition and hormonal health specialists.

Perimenopause Support

Many Ugandan women face perimenopause without any support or information. Our programme demystifies the transition, helps you manage symptoms through diet, movement and lifestyle, and addresses the mental health aspects — because hormonal shifts profoundly affect mood and identity.
