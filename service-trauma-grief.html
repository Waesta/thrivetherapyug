Trauma & Grief

You Don't Have to
Carry This Alone

Trauma-informed therapy and grief support in Kampala. Our licensed therapists create a safe, compassionate space where healing — real healing — is possible.

Understanding Trauma

Trauma is not defined by the event — it's defined by its impact on your nervous system and psyche. Something doesn't have to be dramatic or "big enough" to be traumatic. Abuse, neglect, abandonment, sudden loss, accidents, assault, witnessing violence — all can leave lasting imprints that affect how you feel, think and connect with others.

In Uganda, cultural and social pressures often mean trauma goes unacknowledged and unprocessed. Our therapists understand this context and provide a space where your experience is honoured, never minimised.

Types of Trauma We Work With

  • Childhood trauma and abuse — physical, emotional, sexual abuse and neglect
  • Post-Traumatic Stress Disorder (PTSD) — flashbacks, nightmares, hypervigilance, avoidance
  • Complex trauma (C-PTSD) — repeated or prolonged trauma, often from childhood
  • Relational trauma — toxic relationships, domestic abuse, coercive control
  • Community and collective trauma — conflict, displacement, social violence
  • Medical trauma — difficult diagnoses, surgeries, birth trauma
  • Grief and bereavement — sudden death, prolonged illness, loss of identity
  • Pregnancy loss — miscarriage, stillbirth and the grief that surrounds it

Our Trauma-Informed Approach

We use evidence-based trauma therapies including Trauma-Focused CBT (TF-CBT), EMDR-informed techniques, narrative therapy and somatic (body-focused) approaches. All our therapists are trauma-informed, meaning they understand how trauma lives in the body and shapes every aspect of experience.

Healing from trauma is not linear and there is no timeline. Our therapists work at your pace, in your rhythm, respecting your readiness at every step.

Grief & Bereavement Support

Grief is not a problem to be solved — it's a love with nowhere to go. Our grief-trained counsellors help you move through loss without rushing it, finding meaning and integration over time. We work with sudden loss, prolonged illness, suicide loss, pregnancy loss and anticipatory grief.

Children & Adolescent Trauma

Specialist trauma therapy for children and teenagers, using age-appropriate, play-informed and narrative approaches. We also work with parents to understand their child's trauma responses and respond helpfully.
