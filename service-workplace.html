Workplace Wellness

Healthy Employees.
Thriving Organisations.

Workplace mental health and wellness programmes for Kampala organisations. From burnout prevention to psychological safety training — we help your team thrive.

Why Workplace Wellness Matters

Employee mental health is not a soft issue — it's a business imperative. Burnout, anxiety, depression and toxic workplace dynamics cost Ugandan organisations billions in lost productivity, absenteeism and staff turnover every year. Yet most organisations offer little or no mental health support to their employees.

At Thrive Therapy & Wellness, we partner with organisations in Kampala and across Uganda to build mentally healthy workplaces — through training, workshops, individual support and consulting.

What We Offer Organisations

Employee Wellness Workshops

Half-day and full-day workshops delivered at your premises or online. Topics include: mental health literacy, stress and burnout prevention, communication and conflict management, building psychological safety, and emotional intelligence for leaders and teams.

Employee Assistance Programme (EAP) Consulting

We help HR teams design and implement employee mental health support systems — including confidential referral pathways, crisis protocols and wellness policies that actually work in the Ugandan context.

Manager Mental Health Training

Equipping team leaders with the skills to recognise mental health challenges in their team, have supportive conversations and make appropriate referrals. One of the highest-impact investments an organisation can make.

Individual Support for Employees

Organisations can partner with Thrive to offer their employees access to subsidised individual therapy sessions — a concrete benefit that demonstrates genuine care for employee wellbeing.

Stress & Burnout Assessments

Team-level assessments using validated tools to identify stress hotspots, burnout risk and organisational health — with actionable recommendations for leaders.

Industries We Serve

We work with organisations across Kampala and Uganda including NGOs, banks, healthcare providers, schools and universities, tech companies and government agencies. We understand the unique pressures of each sector.

Why Thrive?

Our team brings clinical expertise, public health experience and a deep understanding of Ugandan workplace culture. We don't deliver generic, imported content — we work with you to create solutions that fit your organisation, your people and your context.
